📱 Campus ID in Apple Wallet

Let's Talk About Bringing This to Your Campus

Apple Wallet lets students, faculty, and staff carry their campus ID right on iPhone and Apple Watch. No more worrying about a lost or forgotten card: a simple tap gets students into dorms, libraries, and campus events, and pays for laundry, snacks, and meals nearby, on and off campus.

A growing number of colleges and universities have already added campus ID to Apple Wallet. Duke University is one example: they rolled this out campuswide, adding the DukeCard to Student ID in Apple Wallet for a campus that sees about 100,000 people a day across more than 250 buildings. As Tracy Futhey, Duke's VP for Information Technology & CIO, put it:

"By pushing the boundaries with our technology, what we're ultimately doing is helping the university achieve its mission, while providing a great experience for everybody at Duke."

Read Duke's full story →

Interested in bringing this to your institution? Standing this up takes some coordination, including card office systems, app integration, and a few conversations to get the right teams connected. If you'd like to explore this idea, reach out to me directly and I'll help get you connected to the right people on the Apple Wallet team.

Tools Worth Knowing

Claris Pulse — Ask Questions About Your Fleet in Plain-English

Claris Pulse is an AI data assistant that connects to your data, MDM exports, and ticketing systems, then answers plain-English questions with charts and downloadable summaries. No formulas or prompt-engineering required. It now connects directly to Apple School Manager data as of June 15, and is already in use at several higher ed institutions. One school district reported cutting a state accountability file comparison from hours down to 12 minutes. Learn more →

Orchard View — One Dashboard for Jamf Pro, Jamf School, and ASM/ABM

Orchard View is a native Mac app that brings device visibility from Jamf Pro, Jamf School, and Apple School/Business Manager together into a single dashboard, alongside your existing MDM. It covers unified inventory, live AppleCare warranty tracking, installed app inventory, and remote lock/restart/erase commands, all deduplicated across sources by serial number. Learn more →

In the News

⚠️ Encrypted HFS+ Support Ending in macOS 28

This one's about a year out, but worth planning for now, especially since some Macs have already started showing a related notification. Per Apple: "In macOS 28 and later, the Mac OS Extended file system format will be supported only for volumes (disks and other storage devices) that aren't encrypted. For future macOS compatibility, either decrypt or reformat any encrypted Mac OS Extended volumes."

Starting with macOS 26, a Mac might notify a user that they're using an encrypted Mac OS Extended (HFS+) disk and that it won't be compatible with macOS 28 or later. Unencrypted Mac OS Extended volumes will continue to work fine. If your users start asking what that notification means, this is it.

Read the full support article →

Apple Platform Security had a number of updates this week, including some new support articles: Terminal and script protections, Search on iCloud.com security, SharePlay security, and Nearby sharing security. See the Intro to Apple platform security guide for more.

⚠️ SentinelOne False-Positive Warning on macOS 26.6

If your institution runs SentinelOne, be aware of reports of false positives on macOS 26.6. As always, we won't detail exploit or vulnerability specifics here — check with SentinelOne directly for current guidance and continue with your normal patch cycle. Apple security releases →
